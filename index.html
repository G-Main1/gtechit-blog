Short answer: The engagement starts with a free consultation. The team then discusses your goals, assesses your needs, and outlines a path forward before any work is committed. Delivery is followed by ongoing support — on-demand helpdesk or a full AMC.

  1. Book a free consultation. Call +977 9743828726, email info@gtechit.com.np, or visit the Dharan-9 office during working hours. There is no cost and no obligation.
  2. Goals and assessment. The team works out what you are trying to achieve and assesses what you currently run — hardware, network, software and data — before proposing anything. This is what stops a solution being sold that does not fit the problem.
  3. Path forward. You get a recommended approach and scope. Engineering work is delivered to enterprise standards, with certified engineers and the relevant vendor certifications behind it.
